FAQ

Straight answers.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Everything saves locally in your browser. Add to home screen and it behaves like an app. Account sync is coming for Pro.

Can I change exercises in Today's Session?

Yes — the table is just a starter Push Day. Swap any row, it persists for today, and your PRs track whatever you log.

How is the streak calculated?

A day counts if you log at least one lift or one supplement. Today is forgiven until midnight — yesterday's streak stays alive until you miss it.

Is my data private?

100% localStorage. Nothing leaves your device. Clear storage = reset. We don't sell supplements, we log them.

Dark mode?

Toggle top-right. It remembers your choice and respects system preference on first visit.
